Chapter 4: The Descent

Ethan hesitated at the edge of the chasm. The air was thick with the scent of ozone and crushed stone, a stark contrast to the sterile environment of the upper levels. He adjusted the straps of his pack, the weight a constant reminder of the supplies he'd secured—or stolen, depending on who you asked.

"We don't have all day," Lyra's voice crackled through the comms. "The perimeter sensors are already resetting."

He knew she was right. The Syndicate wouldn't wait. Taking a deep breath, he stepped into the void, the familiar hum of the repulsor boots kicking in just before he hit terminal velocity.
