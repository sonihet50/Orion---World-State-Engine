Chapter 2

Descent into the Archives

The air in the lower levels tasted of dust and static electricity. Kaelen adjusted his rebreather, the soft hum of its filtration unit the only sound cutting through the oppressive silence of the Undercity Archives. He held the luminator high, its pale beam cutting swathes through the gloom, illuminating rows of crystalline storage matrices that stretched into the abyss.

"Any signal yet?" asked Lyra, her voice crackling over the comms link. She was stationed three levels above, monitoring the structural integrity of the sector.

"Negative. The interference is getting stronger the deeper I go. It's like the walls themselves are absorbing the telemetry." Kaelen paused, running a gloved hand along a shattered interface console. The metal was cold, devoid of the residual heat that usually lingered in these ancient systems. He needed to find the Resonance Cipher before the automated defense grids initiated their next cycle.

Suddenly, a low vibration shuddered through the deck plating. Dust cascaded from the unseen ceiling high above. Kaelen froze, his hand dropping instinctively to the hilt of his pulse-blade. The vibration wasn't random; it was a rhythmic pulse, a slow, deliberate heartbeat echoing through the metallic veins of the facility.

"Lyra," he whispered, eyes scanning the shadows. "Tell me you're reading that."
